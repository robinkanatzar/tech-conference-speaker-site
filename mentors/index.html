Be a mentor for a new speaker

You've spoken at a developer conference. Volunteer a little of your time to help an iOS developer give their talk.

What mentoring involves

After you write to me, we hop on a call so I can get to know you and what you like to help with. Then I pair you with one speaker at a time. The two of you decide how to work together and how much time it takes: a single call to talk through an idea, comments on a proposal, or a practice run before the conference.

You don't need to be a well-known speaker. If you've been through it once, you already know things a first-time speaker doesn't.
