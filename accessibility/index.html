Accessibility

This site should work for everyone, whatever device or assistive technology you use.

What I aim for

I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA on every page.

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, and you can always see where the focus is.
  • Pages have headings, landmarks and a link to skip to the content, for screen readers.
  • Text has strong contrast in light and dark mode, and follows your browser's text size.
  • Pages work at 400% zoom and on a small phone without scrolling sideways.
  • The site follows your settings for dark mode, more contrast, high contrast colours and reduced motion.
  • Every form field has a visible label, and buttons and links are at least 44 pixels tall.
  • There are no cookies, trackers, pop-ups or time limits.

How it was tested

I last reviewed the site on 27 November 2026. Every page was checked with the axe-core testing tool in light and dark mode, at desktop and phone widths, and by keyboard.

Automated tools only find some problems. The site has not yet been tested by people who use a screen reader or voice control every day, so there may be issues I haven't found.

Things to know

  • The forms open a draft in your email app. If no email app opens, your answers appear on the page so you can copy them into any email service.
  • Long answers in the forms are limited to 600 characters. If you need more room, just email me.

Tell me about a problem

If something on this site doesn't work for you, please tell me. Say which page it was and what you were using, and I'll fix it.
