Why this site exists

My first talk? Pure terror. But after I survived it, I realized something: speaking at conferences isn't nearly as impossible as it seems. That realization changed my career.

I've mentored new speakers and written a book about it, and I believe that if I can get on stage, anyone can. You've got the expertise. Someone who has done it before can help you share it from the stage.

This site used to offer my own coaching. It's now an exchange: experienced speakers volunteer as mentors, new speakers ask for one, and I match them after a call with each.
