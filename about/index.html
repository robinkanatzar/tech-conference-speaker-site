Hi, I'm Robin

I'm a software engineer, conference speaker and mentor for new speakers. For the past few years, I've been mentoring new speakers for the Swift Connection conference in Paris, as well as other iOS conferences in Europe and around the world.

I love helping people give their first talk on stage. Giving my own first talk was such a scary experience, but once I did it, I had all of this confidence. I knew that I could share my knowledge with the community and speak again.

I believe it's so valuable to have new voices on stage, sharing their knowledge and experiences with the developer community. I'm here doing everything I can to help bring new voices to the stage.
