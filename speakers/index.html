Get a mentor for your talk

For iOS developers who want to speak at a developer conference. Free, and open to first-time speakers and experienced ones.

What you get

After you write to me, we hop on a call to talk about where you are and what you need. Then I pair you with a volunteer mentor: someone who has given conference talks and wants to help you give yours. You can ask for help with any of this:

  • Finding a talk idea
  • Writing a proposal for a call for papers (CFP)
  • Choosing which conferences to apply to
  • Structuring the talk and its story
  • Slides and live demos
  • Rehearsing, delivery and nerves
  • Preparing for questions from the audience

You don't need a finished talk, an accepted proposal or even an idea to ask.
